About

The analytics were rarely the hard part.

For twenty years I worked where data meets a decision — across billion-dollar capital projects, offshore joint ventures, a national retail network, and a US$40 billion global supply chain. The pattern was always the same. Building the model, the pipeline, the dashboard — that was the manageable part. Getting people to own the data, agree what a number actually meant, and then act on it — that was the work.

So that's the work I do now. I advise organisations on data strategy and governance, write about the human side of decision-making, and run a learning community for people who would rather think a problem through than reach for the nearest dashboard. The through-line, on every surface: AI can sharpen a decision, but a person still has to make it — and answer for it.

Off the clock: a committed sci-fi reader and a hands-on learner — the kind who understands a new tool best by building something real with it.
